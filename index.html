About us

Open source software is code that anyone can read, use and improve. Linux, Firefox, Python and most of the tools you use for your degree are built this way, by people all over the world working in public.

We're a student society at the University of Manchester for anyone who wants to get involved. You don't need to have contributed before, or even be on a computing course. We'll help you make your first contribution and meet people who like building things.

What we do

  • Workshops

    Hands-on sessions on Git, Linux, the command line and the tools real projects use.

  • Contribution sprints

    Pick an issue on a real project and work through it with others in the room to help.

  • Talks

    Maintainers and engineers on how open source works in practice and in industry.

  • Socials

    Food, games and a chance to meet the people behind the Discord usernames.
