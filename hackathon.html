What's a hackathon?

A hackathon is an event where you team up and build something from scratch in a short, fixed time. It could be an app, a tool, a game or something that barely works but is fun to show off.

You don't need to be an expert. Plenty of people come to their first hackathon to learn, and there will be mentors around to help you get unstuck.

        FAQ

        Who can take part?

        Any University of Manchester student, on any course and at any level.

        How much does it cost?

        Nothing. It's free to take part.

        I've never done a hackathon. Can I still come?

        Yes. Beginners are very welcome, and we'll have mentors on hand to help.

        Do I need a team?

        No. You can come with friends or find a team on the day.

        What should I bring?

        A laptop, a charger and your student ID.

        Will there be food?

        Details coming soon. Tell us about any dietary needs when you register.

        Is the venue accessible?

        We'll share full access information once the venue is confirmed. If you have any questions, email us and we'll help.

        Is there a code of conduct?

        Yes. We want everyone to feel welcome, and we'll publish our code of conduct before registration opens.
