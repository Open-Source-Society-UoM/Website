What's a hackathon?

A hackathon is an event where you team up and build something from scratch in a short, fixed time. It could be an app, a tool, a game or something that barely works but is fun to show off.

You don't need to be an expert. Plenty of people come to their first hackathon to learn, and there will be mentors around to help you get unstuck.

This one is part of Hacktoberfest, the yearly celebration of open source that runs through October. The day starts with an intro to making your first contribution, so it's a good place to start if you've never opened a pull request.

        FAQ

        Who can take part?

        Any university student. You don't have to study computing, and you don't have to be at Manchester.

        How much does it cost?

        Nothing. It's free to take part and lunch is provided.

        How do I sign up?

        Register through MLH using the button at the top of this page. Spots are limited.

        I've never done a hackathon. Can I still come?

        Yes. There's an intro to open source contribution at 12:30 and mentors around all day to help you get unstuck.

        Do I need a team?

        No. You can come with friends or find people on the day.

        What should I bring?

        A laptop, a charger and your student ID. It helps to pick out a few good first issues beforehand, and there are some linked from our Discord.

        Will there be food?

        Lunch is served at 13:00. Tell us about any dietary needs when you register.

        Where exactly is it?

        The SU Theatre on the ground floor of the Steve Biko Building, Oxford Road, Manchester M13 9PR. If you have any access questions, email us and we'll help.

        Is there a code of conduct?

        Yes. This is an MLH event, so everyone taking part agrees to the MLH code of conduct.
